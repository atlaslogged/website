Common Questions

Why is location permission needed?

Atlas Logged provides automatic location change tracking to give you insights into your travel history. Location permissions allow the app to track your movements in the background while using minimal battery.

Can anyone access my location data?

No. Atlas Logged keeps your data on-device with optional privacy settings to reduce stored data to only Country and City names. Data can only be exported manually by you.

How does this affect battery life?

Atlas Logged uses Apple's native 'on location change' events, so the app only activates when you actually move. Even on heavy travel days, battery usage is typically less than 1%.
