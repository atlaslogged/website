What's New

A running record of new features, improvements, and fixes in Atlas Logged.

v1.2.0August 22, 2026 Our Biggest Update Yet ⌄

New features

  • Multi-Device Support

    Use Atlas Logged across all your devices with seamless sync

  • iCloud Backup

    Your travel history is now safely backed up to iCloud

  • Photo History

    See your photos alongside your country stays and travel timeline

  • Feedback & Support

    New feedback screen to easily get in touch with us

  • Time Zones, Done Right

    Each day now knows the time zone you lived it in: photos line up with your travel days, and a new Time Zone card shows the local time there versus your time now.

  • Home Nations, Day by Day

    Trips around the UK now show England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland per day - a drive from Wales into England shows both, in order.

  • Device Fleet Management

    Declare the devices you use, claim this one from the list, and rename, retire or hand over the primary role from Settings.

Improvements

  • Onboarding Experience

    Completely redesigned setup flow to get you started faster

  • Calendar Reliability

    Stable month navigation and background refresh without snapping back

  • Performance

    Faster app launch and smoother navigation throughout

  • Switching to a New iPhone

    Upgrading moves your setup and history over cleanly - and the app now recognises your phone even when the system misreports its storage size.

  • Editing Your Stays

    Swipe to edit, hide or delete stays in your travel history, with clearer handling while history is being recalculated.

Bug fixes

  • Tracking Recovery

    Location tracking can no longer be silently left off after testing or unusual shutdowns - the app now detects and recovers from GPS outages.

  • Phantom Stays

    Fixed rare cases where the calendar invented days in a country you had already left.

  • Battery

    Fixed a loop that could quietly recalculate your history over and over after a long gap in tracking.

  • Offline Mode

    Choosing “stay offline” during setup now truly stays offline.

  • Photo History

    The photo timeline scan no longer looks stuck at 100% while it finishes up.

  • Privacy Settings

    Clearer wording for the highest-accuracy location level.

  • Calendar & Logging

    Fixed date selection, range validation, and manual log consistency

  • Stability

    Numerous bug fixes and reliability improvements
